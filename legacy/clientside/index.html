About us

Who we are

We fund community health programs for families who cannot afford private care.

How your donation is used

  • 100% of ticket income is directed to the cause named on each event page.
  • Every event publishes a funding goal and a live progress bar.
  • Our accounts are audited annually and published to members.
  • Volunteers cover the running costs so more money reaches the cause.

Hosting your own event?

Community groups, schools and local businesses can partner with us. We provide the event listing, the ticketing structure and the fundraising reporting, and you keep the cause you care about.
